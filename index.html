Paste a monthly FSC rate table, review the mapped origin-to-destination rates, and scan flagged issues — all on one screen. Nothing is saved between visits.

1. Paste Rate Table Paste or edit the monthly FSC rate table text below

Tip: paste directly from your spreadsheet or email. Two formats both work — list rows like Origin Country | Destination Region | Rate, or a full matrix table where the first line lists the destination regions (Greater China, North Asia, ASEAN, South Asia, AU & NZ, Middle East & EU, Africa, Domestic, US) and each line below is one origin country followed by its rates. A dash (—) means no rate is published. Results in Section 2 update automatically as you type.

Simple Reminders

  • Rates shown here are parsed from the text you pasted — always confirm against the official monthly FSC notice before quoting.
  • A dash (—) in the source table means no fuel surcharge is published for that lane; those lanes are listed as "No rate".
  • Check that each destination region uses your team's standard names (Greater China, North Asia, ASEAN, South Asia, AU & NZ, Middle East & EU, Africa, Domestic, US).
  • This page holds nothing between visits: copy results out before closing the tab.
